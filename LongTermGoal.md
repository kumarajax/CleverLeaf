# AI-Powered Online Learning and Examination Platform

============================================================
1. VISION
============================================================

Develop a cloud-based educational platform that enables students
from Grade 3 through Grade 12 to practice, assess, and improve
their academic performance through AI-assisted learning and
examination systems.

The platform will initially focus on school-level subjects and
later expand to competitive examinations such as:

- SAT
- ACT
- CAT
- GRE
- GMAT
- JEE
- NEET
- UPSC
- Professional Certification Exams

The system must support millions of users and eventually handle
large-scale online examinations with up to 2 million concurrent
participants.
