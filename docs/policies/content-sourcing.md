# Content Sourcing Policy

ClearLeaf generates original questions from curriculum concepts and learning
objectives. It must not lightly rewrite commercial questions by changing
numbers, names, answers, or wording.

## Required controls

- Use official curriculum documents, licensed sources, or public-domain sources.
- Record source reference, URL where applicable, license category, learning
  objective, and generation metadata.
- Extract concepts and coverage gaps rather than reusable question text.
- Reject copied explanations, passages, diagrams, and distinctive structures.
- Apply similarity checks before publication.
- Require human approval before a question becomes `READY_FOR_TEST`.
- Obtain legal review before monetization or scheduled crawling.
