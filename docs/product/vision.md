# ClearLeaf Vision

ClearLeaf is an AI-assisted learning and examination preparation platform.
Phase 1 is a local private pilot for CBSE/NCERT Grade 5 and 6 Math and Science.
The long-term vision in `LongTermGoal.md` remains authoritative for future
phases, including competitive examinations and high-scale scheduled exams.

## Phase 1 principles

- Local-first deployment with cloud-portable interfaces.
- Modular monolith before independently deployed business services.
- Configurable curriculum taxonomy with historical retention.
- Original AI-assisted content with mandatory human review.
- No automatic crawling or publication in Phase 1.
